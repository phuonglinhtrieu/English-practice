DẠNG 1: TRẮC NGHIỆM TỪ VỰNG & NGỮ PHÁP (CÂU 1 - 8)
1. You shouldn't ______ doctor's advice when you have a fever.
2. Washing hands frequently helps to prevent the ______ of diseases.
3. I have learned English ______ 3 years ago.
4. She has played the piano ______ 5 months.
5. If I were you, I ______ more vegetables every day.
6. He is ______ tired that he cannot finish his work.
7. She runs ______ fast that nobody can catch her.
8. I have no idea ______ to fix this broken bike.
DẠNG 2: CHỌN LƯỢNG TỪ THÍCH HỢP (CÂU 9 - 16)
9. There is only ______ water left in the bottle. (1 ít - N không đếm được)
10. He bought ______ apples at the supermarket. (1 vài - N đếm được)
11. She has ______ friends because she is very shy. (Rất ít - N đếm được)
12. There is ______ sugar in my tea. It is not sweet. (Rất ít - N không đếm được)
13. I have ______ books about healthy food. (Một vài - N đếm được)
14. They spent ______ money on fast food. (Rất nhiều)
15. My mother eats ______ fresh oranges every week. (Nhiều)
16. We need ______ minutes to finish this exercise. (Một vài)
DẠNG 3: CHIA DẠNG ĐÚNG CỦA ĐỘNG TỪ (CÂU 17 - 23)

18. My father stops ______ (smoke) because it is bad for health.
19. She is very good at ______ (cook) healthy meals.
20. If it rains tomorrow, we ______ (cancel) the outdoor match.
21. Nam is bad at ______ (play) football.
22. They stopped ______ (take) photos when they saw the lake.
23. If she works hard, she ______ (pass) the exam easily.
